#  Project Sunrise

Hey there! Welcome to **Project Sunrise**—a passion project of mine focused on building a next-gen, enterprise-grade aerospace intelligence and multi-fleet 3D digital twin platform. 

I built this to bridge the gap between complex aircraft telemetry and intuitive, real-time visualization. Whether you're tracking structural strains or looking at deep learning anomaly predictions, everything is packed into a sleek, dark-themed dashboard.

---

##  What's Inside?

- **Interactive 3D Digital Twins:** Powered by Three.js, you get a real-time 3D wireframe representation of the aircraft (fuselage, wings, stabilizers) right in your browser with smooth spatial synchronization.
- **Multi-Fleet Support:** Easily switch between a wide range of commercial aircraft, including major Airbus models (`A350-1000`, `A321XLR`, `A380-800`) and Boeing models (`B787-9`, `B737MAX`, `B777-9`, `B777-300ER`).
- **Autonomous AI Diagnostic Engine:** Features a built-in anomaly classification system to evaluate structural fatigue, thermal equilibrium, and critical system health.
- **Deep Telemetry Monitoring:** Keep an eye on vital components like composite barrel joint strain gages, EBAS (Electric Bleed Air System), hydraulic pressures, and avionics.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Three.js, Custom CSS Dark UI
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python, Uvicorn
- **Deployment:** Vercel (Frontend) & Render (Backend)
